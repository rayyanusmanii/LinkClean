# Privacy Policy for LinkClean

**Last Updated:** December 25, 2025

LinkClean ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles your data.

## 1. Data Collection
LinkClean does not store, collect, sell, or share any personal user data.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account to use this extension.
* **No Tracking:** We do not track your browsing history, search queries, or personal interactions on LinkedIn.

## 2. How Your Data is Used
The extension functions by reading the text content of LinkedIn posts visible on your screen.
* **Processing:** This text is sent to our secure server-side API (Google Cloud Run) solely for the purpose of classification (determining if a post is a job opportunity or not).
* **No Storage:** The text is processed in real-time and immediately discarded. It is never saved to a database or used to train AI models.

## 3. Third-Party Services
* **Google Cloud Platform:** We use Google Cloud Run to host our classification logic. Google Cloud adheres to strict security and privacy standards.

## 4. Contact
If you have questions about this policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support tab.
